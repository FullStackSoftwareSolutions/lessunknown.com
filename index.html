About

Governance, not labor

Independent oversight for physical layer and connectivity programs across large pharmaceutical campuses, from design intent through closeout.

For twenty-five years, large pharmaceutical organizations — multi-campus, multi-building, multi-floor — have been essentially my only work. I operate as an independent consultant providing physical layer and connectivity governance for these environments. My role is to apply standards, define constraints, and set acceptance criteria so infrastructure is designed correctly, remains maintainable, and retains long-term value. I'm not present during installation and don't manage labor — my authority sits at design intent, governance, and quality verification.

I approach connectivity as a system, not a collection of tickets. Copper, fiber, pathways, rooms, racks, patching, labeling, testing, and documentation are treated as one interdependent environment, so Ethernet, PoE, CCTV, intercoms, clocks, BAS, A/V systems, and industrial instruments function as a coherent whole rather than disconnected installations.

Governance Competencies

How I operate

Twenty-five years of retrofit and build experience across large pharmaceutical campuses, distilled into a repeatable governance framework.

Independent Governance

I set design intent, constraints, and acceptance criteria without directing crews or managing day-to-day site activity — complementing project management rather than replacing it.

Systems Thinking

Connectivity is treated as one interdependent environment — cabling, pathways, testing, and documentation working together, not as disconnected installations.

Outcome-Driven Strategy

Constraints and acceptance criteria are defined upfront to reduce ambiguity during execution and prevent reactive correction after the fact.

Coordination Without Overreach

I coordinate expectations with contractors and stakeholders and verify quality at closeout against design intent, testing results, and documentation.

Long-Term Risk Reduction

Standards, alignment, and documentation discipline protect organizations from operational risk, future outages, and loss of institutional knowledge due to turnover.

Organizational Fluency

I work within real constraints — competing stakeholders, budget pressure, trade sequencing, and continuity requirements — while preserving contractor autonomy.

Technical Credibility

Lifelong familiarity with data centres and telecom distribution facilities, working fluently with ANSI/TIA and BICSI standards and industry-standard tooling.

DCIM & Software Architecture

Documentation only holds value if it stays accurate. I contribute to DCIM and financial software architecture so systems reflect how operations actually function.

Disciplined Multitasking

The skill I rely on most: running multiple buildings, stakeholders, and workstreams in parallel — not sequencing tasks, but holding all of them at once, without dropping a detail or introducing an error.

Technical Programs

Selected work

Representative programs spanning retrofit, new build, security integration, and infrastructure rationalization within large pharmaceutical, multi-campus environments.

01

Multi-Campus Connectivity Retrofit

Developed and deployed a proprietary DCIM and connectivity management platform (eTRACS) to support structured cabling upgrades across more than a hundred telecom rooms, linking CAD documentation with SQL databases for a consistent framework from design through invoicing.

02

Data Centre Constructions

Guided the physical layer and connectivity scope during the build of two large data centres, aligning backbone cabling, distribution systems, and testing with IT and facilities requirements across consultants, contractors, and stakeholders.

03

Fiber Reroute & Transition Design

Directed the rerouting of legacy single-mode fiber through existing underground pathways into a new utility structure, designing the transition from armored OSP cabling to inside-plant terminations with contingency planning for MPO trunk replacement.

04

Security Operations Centre Cabling

Produced structured cabling designs for a new SOC, including copper distribution, optical fiber pathways, CCTV integration, intercoms, and wireless access point connectivity, applying ANSI/TIA and BICSI standards.

05

Axis CCTV & IP Security Integration

Worked with Axis Communications equipment and security contractors to integrate IP-based CCTV, intercoms, and video telephones into structured cabling, designing PoE delivery for long-term maintainability.

06

KVM Infrastructure Rationalization

Reassessed a proposed high-cost KVM solution against existing interconnections and operational history, delivering a leaner, standards-compliant design that reduced overall cost.

07

CCTV & PoE Delivery Clarification

Provided design clarification to security contractors on proper use of mid-span PoE injectors for surveillance equipment, keeping documentation aligned with structured cabling best practices.

08

Vendor Oversight & Infrastructure Auditing

Managed vendor compliance with safety, health, and technical standards, and audited labor, material costs, and documentation deliverables to ensure quality and best-value results.
